Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This policy explains how Adana.fm collects, uses, and protects your information.

Data Collection

Adana.fm collects information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and any information submitted through contact forms or newsletter sign-ups.

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit the site, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and general usage analytics.

Use of Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Send newsletters and updates about new music and shows
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form
  • Improve site functionality and user experience
  • Monitor and maintain the security of the site

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Third-Party Services

This site embeds content and functionality from third-party platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and Bandsintown. These platforms operate independently and maintain their own privacy policies governing any data they collect.

Cookies

Adana.fm uses cookies to support site functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may withdraw consent to data processing at any time. International users may be entitled to additional protections regarding cross-border data transfers.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention & Children's Privacy

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. This site is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
